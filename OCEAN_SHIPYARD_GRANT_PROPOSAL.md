## 1. Executive Summary
OpticParse is an open-source, edge-native computer vision web scraper and threat intelligence engine. It runs **150 continuous extraction pipelines** at the edge across 13 major industries (Cybersecurity, E-Commerce, Finance, Crypto, and B2B Signals), harvesting over **1,250 verified telemetry records every 24 hours** into a permanent data lake on Cloudflare R2 and Vectorize.

Through this grant, OpticParse will establish a **first-class continuous data pipeline for the Ocean Protocol ecosystem**, enabling AI agents, decentralized research DAOs, and Web3 security providers to consume, stake on, and train algorithms via Ocean Compute-to-Data (C2D).

---

## 2. Problem Statement
* **Traditional scrapers are brittle:** DOM obfuscation and anti-bot systems break standard CSS scrapers.
* **AI training requires continuous, fresh telemetry:** AI agents and cyber defense models need clean, structured, time-series data, not stale 2-year-old static CSV dumps.
* **Decentralized AI lacks real-time web telemetry:** Web3 AI protocols (such as Bittensor, SingularityNET, and Ocean Predictoor) lack autonomous multi-modal edge pipelines that feed them live, structured data continuously.

## 3. The OpticParse Solution on Ocean Protocol
OpticParse tokenizes its 150-template Brain into **4 distinct Data NFT feeds** on Polygon:
1. **`OPTIC-MASTER-150`**: Unified multi-industry multimodal vision training corpus.
2. **`OPTIC-PHISH`**: Zero-day phishing kit hashes, wallet-drainer dApps, and brand impersonation telemetry.
3. **`OPTIC-ECOM`**: Real-time retail pricing arbitrage, Amazon/Shopify stock drop alerts.
4. **`OPTIC-LEADS`**: Tech founder funding signals and developer hiring telemetry.
